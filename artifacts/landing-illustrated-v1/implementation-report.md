## Layout delivered (browser build, route `/`)

1. **Forest hero** (`landing-dark`): `hero.webp` full-bleed, eager/high priority. Copy, Get started and
   "See how it works" on the dark left; the section is 2:1 from `lg` (min 560 / max 860 px) with
   `object-position: center 78%`, so the 3:2 scene keeps faces and boots in view (E2E asserts the
   visible crop never exceeds 2.05:1). Below `lg` the copy comes first, then the scene at 1:1 / 4:3
   with a right-of-centre crop that keeps the two main scouts whole. One image, no card, no compass.
2. **Cream steps** (`landing-cream`, cream in both themes): "A little planning. A lot of adventure."
   full-width heading; 01 / 02 / 03 Build your game, Explore together, Make every stop count;
   `step-plan/explore/checkin.webp` under the copy, no boxes; stacked on phones.
3. **Organizer band** (`landing-dark`, plain evergreen background): "Out in the field. Always in the
   loop." + Get started; a stage where `workspace-preview.webp` (Costa de Lavos fixture) takes the
   right 80 % in a framed screen and `guide-pointing.webp` stands at 34 % width on the same baseline,
   overlapping the screen's lower-left edge. Caption plus OpenStreetMap / CARTO attribution links.
   Background, guide and screenshot are three independent layers.
4. **Pricing** (compact, theme-aware): original strings, prices, yearly/monthly toggle
   (`role=group`, `aria-pressed`), organizer-gate routes, club `mailto:` contact. No invented copy.
5. **Forest call to action**: `forest-footer.webp` background with a bottom scrim, "Your next
   adventure starts here.", Get started, "Playing today? Get the app" iOS / Android links
   (`landing-download-ios/android`), footer with brand, tagline, Pricing / FAQ / Privacy / Login.

Header: existing brand mark, How it works / For organizers / Pricing, language select, theme toggle
(shares `pointfinder-theme`), Login, Get started, phone menu (`aria-expanded`, focus into the menu,
Escape returns focus, closes on link click / breakpoint). Skip link to `#main`.

Preserved: the 220 ms fade into `/welcome` (immediate under reduced motion, modified clicks untouched),
`Start free` → `/welcome?role=organizer`, `.landing-page` opacity transition asserted by the existing
account-onboarding spec, login / store / FAQ / privacy / contact routes.

## Gaps and notes

- The root-level `e2e/` smoke (`./run.sh smoke:web`) needs the Docker stack and does not visit `/`;
  it was not run. The web-level Playwright specs above are the smoke coverage for the homepage.
- `index.html` still loads Fraunces / Geist / JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts; the homepage now uses
  only the UI sans (Geist → Inter fallback). Removing Fraunces from `index.html` is out of scope.
- The App Store / Google Play badges from the interim version are gone by design; the final CTA uses
  the concept's text links.
- Reduced motion: hero reveal and the welcome fade are disabled; nothing else animates.
- Root visual review passed five viewport/language/theme cases. Root then made one CSS-only tweak
  (`.landing-cream` primary = 70 % evergreen + mint, so step numbers stay a dark readable green in the
  dark theme); I left it in place and did not re-run the broad suites afterwards at root's request.
- I have not touched `artifacts/landing-illustrated-v1/review/`.
