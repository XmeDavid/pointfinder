# Personal experience imagery

`coastal-trail.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-10. It is atmospheric illustrative artwork, not a photograph documenting the game's actual location. Forest and terrain assets are reused from the existing homepage.

Final prompt:

> Create a polished wide landscape illustration for a mobile location-based exploration app, 1536x1024. A coastal footpath on the Atlantic coast of Portugal near Costa de Lavos: pale sand dunes with low coastal grasses, a modest white lighthouse in the distance, calm muted blue-green ocean, windswept pine at right edge, warm late afternoon soft sun. Restrained mature tactile 3D environmental illustration, softly modeled natural forms, rich forest green accents, subtle cinematic depth. More like a crafted landscape than a cartoon. No people, no characters, no toys, no glowing markers, no lettering, no logos, no UI. Full bleed composition, focal interest across middle horizontal band so usable as a panoramic crop. Beautiful inviting place to explore, quiet and grounded.
